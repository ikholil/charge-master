`Offline battery & charging analytics with live power, ETA, and capacity estimates.` *(80 chars)*

### Full Description (max 4000 chars)
```text
Know your battery. Charge smarter.

Charge Master is an offline-first, privacy-first battery and charging analytics app. It measures what Android exposes through public APIs, explains every metric clearly, and never invents values—when a sensor isn't available on your device, the app tells you.

KEY FEATURES

• Live Dashboard
View battery level, charging status, power source (AC, USB, wireless, dock), temperature, voltage, current, estimated power (V × A), Android operational health status, thermal status, and time estimates (time to 80%, time to full, or remaining time on battery).

• Charging Monitor & Session History
Automatically records charging sessions on your device with start/end levels, average and peak power, temperature range, source, and interruptions. View a charging calendar, fastest and slowest sessions, USB vs. AC comparisons, power vs. level, and temperature vs. power charts.

• Battery Health & Capacity Estimates
Separates Android's operational battery status from State of Health. Estimates full-charge capacity from charge-counter deltas over qualifying sessions (30%+ charge span), flags fuel gauges derived from percentage, and shows an estimated health percentage when you enter your battery's rated design capacity. Displays Android 14+ cycle count when reported by your device.

• Temperature & Consumption Insights
Track battery temperature history with min/max/average while charging vs. discharging and switch between °C and °F. Review 24h, 7d, and 30d discharge rates, screen-on vs. screen-off drain, and monitoring coverage.

• Charging Habit Score
See how often your recent sessions stayed near your chosen charge target, avoided deep discharges, stayed below your temperature limit, and avoided overly long charges. Describes your charging habits using your own thresholds.

• Smart Alerts
Optional notifications for low battery, 80% / 90% / 100% charge targets, charging started/complete/interrupted, high temperature, long charging, and unusually slow charging.

• Home Screen Widgets & Quick Settings Tile
Three adaptive Glance widgets (Minimal, Detailed, Charging) plus a Quick Settings tile that update alongside recorded samples without background polling.

• Themes & Languages
System, Light, Dark, and AMOLED themes with dynamic color, high contrast, reduced motion, and full English and Bangla (বাংলা) support.

PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY
• 100% offline: no INTERNET permission, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
• All battery readings, sessions, and settings stay on your device.
• Exportable plain-text diagnostic report contains no personal identifiers.
• Battery history is excluded from cloud backup and can be cleared anytime in Settings.

Note: Sensor availability and accuracy (such as current, charge counter, and cycle count) depend on your device hardware and Android version. Charge Master monitors and estimates—Android does not allow regular apps to stop or limit charging.
```
