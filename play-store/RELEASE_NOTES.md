## Full Release Summary (`v1.0.0`)

- **Live Dashboard:** Battery level, charging status and source (AC, USB, wireless, dock), temperature (°C/°F), voltage, current, estimated power (V × A), Android operational health status, thermal status, and labelled ETA / remaining-time estimates.
- **Charging Monitor & History:** Live power chart, session duration, peak and average power, charging calendar, fastest/slowest sessions, USB vs. AC comparison, and CSV export.
- **Battery Health & Capacity Estimates:** Charge-counter-based capacity estimates over qualifying sessions (30%+ span), optional health % against user-entered design capacity, and Android 14+ cycle count on supported devices.
- **Temperature & Consumption Insights:** Temperature history with gap handling, 24h / 7d / 30d discharge rates, screen-on vs. screen-off drain, and Charging Habit Score.
- **Alerts, Widgets & Personalization:** Configurable battery/temperature alerts, three adaptive Glance widgets, Quick Settings tile, Light / Dark / AMOLED themes, and full English & Bangla (বাংলা) localization.
- **Privacy & Transparency:** Offline-first with no `INTERNET` permission, no ads, no analytics, and an About screen with Hikmahlab studio details.
