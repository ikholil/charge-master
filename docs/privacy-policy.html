Charge Master — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-28

Charge Master does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

On-device only

The app stores the following information strictly on your device:

  • Battery readings: level, temperature, voltage, current, charge counter, charging status and source, and screen on/off state at sample time.
  • Charging sessions and capacity estimates.
  • Your app settings and alert preferences.

This data never leaves your device. The app has no INTERNET permission.

Sharing

If you choose to share a battery diagnostic report, the plain-text report is passed to the app you select in Android's share sheet. It contains battery metrics and your device manufacturer, model, and Android version, and does not contain identifiers such as serial number, IMEI, Android ID, accounts, or location.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used only for battery and charging alerts you enable.
  • Start at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Lets battery monitoring resume and close interrupted sessions after a device restart.

Retention and deletion

Readings are retained for the period you choose (7, 30, 90, or 365 days). You can delete all stored history at any time in Settings › Data › Clear all history. Uninstalling the app removes all data. The battery history database is excluded from Android cloud backup.
