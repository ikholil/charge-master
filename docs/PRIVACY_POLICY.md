# Charge Master — Privacy Policy (draft)

*Last updated: 2026-11-25*

Charge Master does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

**On-device only.** The app stores the following on your device:
- battery readings: level, temperature, voltage, current, charge counter, charging status and source, and screen on/off state at sample time
- charging sessions
- capacity estimates
- your settings

This data never leaves the device. The app has no internet permission.

**Sharing.** If you choose to share a battery report, the text is passed to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. The report contains battery information and your device manufacturer, model and Android version. It does not contain identifiers such as serial number, IMEI, Android ID, accounts or location.

**Permissions.**
- Notifications are used only for alerts you enable.
- Start-at-boot lets monitoring resume after a restart.

**Retention and deletion.** Readings are kept for the period you choose: 7, 30, 90 or 365 days. You can delete everything at any time in Settings › Data. Uninstalling the app removes all data. The battery history database is excluded from cloud backup.
